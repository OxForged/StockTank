# Alerts, follow up, and the LinkedIn honest truth

## Smart Follow-up Engine, your money saver
Warm deals go cold when nobody follows up. This watches every deal in
talks. When one has been quiet for your chosen number of days, 7 by
default, it drafts a friendly follow up through the BD Manager and puts a
high priority task on your list to send it. You just send. Set the days
in Settings, Follow up after how many quiet days. It runs inside Plan my
day, so warm deals never slip away again.

## Proactive Alerts to your phone, free
When the Deals Hunter finds a hot lead or the News Manager catches a big
story, you get a message on your phone. It is free with Telegram.
Setup, five minutes:
1. On Telegram, message BotFather, make a new bot, copy the token.
2. Message your new bot once, then get your chat id (message the bot
   userinfobot to see your id).
3. Paste both in Settings, Phone alerts. Done.
Leave it blank to skip. No WhatsApp on purpose, it needs a paid verified
business account, Telegram does the same for free.

## Daily digest
There is a digest endpoint that builds a plain text brief of your day,
top tasks, new leads, posts waiting, top news. You can read it or wire it
to email yourself. Simple and always available.

## LinkedIn, the honest truth
You asked for LinkedIn company updates and mutual connections. I have to
be straight, there is no legal way to pull that. LinkedIn has no public
API for a prospect company's posts or your mutual connections. Scraping
it, even read only, breaks their terms and risks a ban on your real
account, the one you close deals on. So instead the Deals Hunter gives
you the exact company LinkedIn link on every lead card. You open it as a
human, which is safe and works. That is the honest, safe way, and it is
already built in.
