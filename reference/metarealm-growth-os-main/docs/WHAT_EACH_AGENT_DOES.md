# What each AI employee does, and what it cannot do

Open the AI Team page in the app to see this any time. The one rule that
covers all of them: they write and find things for you. None of them can
post, send, or log into your accounts. You always press send.

## The deal team, your priority

Opportunity Hunter. Finds companies showing a real gaming deal signal,
a brand wanting creators, a team, a campaign, a sponsorship. Every find
keeps its source link. It will never invent a company. If it finds
nothing real, it tells you honestly. You open the source, then their
LinkedIn, to find the person. Needs search on, SearXNG free or a Grok key.

BD Manager. Drafts an outreach email for a company, intro or follow up,
using your real numbers. You copy it, send it from your own inbox, then
press Mark as sent so a touch is logged. It cannot send email itself.

Proposal Builder. Turns a deal into a real Word proposal with your
numbers. You download, review, send. It cannot send anything.

## The content team

Content Strategist. Fill my morning makes 6 tweets, 6 LinkedIn posts, 3
Instagram ideas from your baked in stats. No upload needed. Everything
waits in your approval queue. It cannot post.

## The intel team

Market Intelligence. Collects gaming news, Morocco and MENA first. Free
sources are limited, a Grok key adds live X where most breaks. Bookmark
what you want to keep.

Research Analyst. Digs into one topic and saves a short cited report.

## The daily helpers

Executive Assistant. Writes your morning brief from real data.
Meeting Assistant. Preps you before a meeting.
CRM Manager. Flags stale deals and missing prep, simple rules, no AI.
Relationship Manager. Reminds you to check in with warm partners. It
cannot message anyone or touch your accounts, it only reminds you.

## The honest truth about contacts

No safe, cheap tool gives you a decision maker's email from a company
name. That data hides behind LinkedIn, which bans automated searching.
So the system finds the company and the proof, and you close on LinkedIn
as a human. That is how real deals happen, and it keeps your accounts safe.
