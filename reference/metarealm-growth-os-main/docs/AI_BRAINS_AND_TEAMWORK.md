# Which AI runs each agent, and how they work as a team

## The best brain for each job
Your agents can run on three brains. Free Ollama by default. Add keys in
Settings to switch on the paid ones, and the system routes each agent to
its best brain automatically. No setup beyond pasting the key.

News Manager, best brain Grok. Moroccan and MENA gaming news and drama
break on X first, and only Grok reads live X. Perplexity is a good
second for cited open web news.

Deals Hunter, best brain Grok to find, Claude to judge. Deal signals, a
brand wanting creators, saying are you a gamer, live on X. Grok finds
them, and when a Claude key is also set the writing and judgement get
sharper.

Research Analyst, best brain Grok. Same reason, freshest world context.

Content Agent, best brain Claude. Claude writes the most human, natural
prose. This is where your no AI sounding rule is best respected.

BD Manager and Proposal Builder, best brain Claude. These write the
words clients read, so writing quality matters most.

## My honest recommendation
Add a Grok key for news and deal hunting, a few dollars a month, hard
capped. Add a Claude key for content and outreach. Together they stay
well inside your budget, and each agent uses the tool it is best at.

## How the team works together
The agents are not alone. They share what they find.

1. News Manager collects the news, Morocco first, then MENA, then web3,
   then world drama. It scores every story so the best is on top.
2. Content Agent reads that news and writes posts that react to it, for
   LinkedIn and X, six each. It also uses your baked in facts. Not every
   post is about us, most react to the scene, so you look like the go to.
3. Deals Hunter reads that same news too. If a story names a brand doing
   something in gaming, that becomes a lead, on top of its own live
   searches. Every lead keeps the source link and every way to reach them.
4. BD Manager drafts the intro email for each lead.
5. Chief of Staff, the boss, turns all of it into your daily task list.

You press Plan my day once. The team works in this order, sharing as they
go, and hands you the plan. You execute.
